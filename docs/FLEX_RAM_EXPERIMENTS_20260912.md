## R1 — consume finalized buckets

The production tail consumes a RAM bucket once, transferring its encoded
segments rather than deep-copying them. Encoded buffers are released after
decoding; decoded runs are released after their merge, before correction.
The reusable read methods retain their behavior until consumption; subsequent
RAM reads fail explicitly. Spill reads remain reusable. Bucket-level OpenMP
parallelism, merge ordering, exact-count saturation, region propagation, UMI
correction and downstream matrix/caller processing are unchanged.
